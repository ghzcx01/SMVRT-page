Abstract

We present SMVRT, a novel architecture for Implicit Human 3D Modeling Using Sparse Multi-View Volumetric Reconstruction with Transformer Fusion.

Our approach leverages carefully designed AFM, MMFT, and QFT modules to effectively fuse multi-view features and accurately reconstruct high-fidelity 3D human geometry even under sparse camera setups. By combining the strengths of volumetric representation and Transformer-based feature aggregation, SMVRT achieves state-of-the-art performance on challenging human reconstruction benchmarks.
